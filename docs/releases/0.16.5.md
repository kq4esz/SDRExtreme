# Signal Outpost 0.16.5

## Clickable RF waterfall and wide survey

- Tune by clicking a signal in the Live Radio or Decoders waterfall.
- Zoom in and out with the controls or mouse wheel; wheel zoom follows the pointer.
- Use Wide survey for a 0–2.4 GHz panorama. An RTL-SDR scans supported tuner bands sequentially, so audio pauses while surveying and coverage takes time.
- Unsupported frequencies are marked, and the survey restores the previous tuned frequency when stopped.
